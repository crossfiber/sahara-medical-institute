Dr. Francisco Peraza

Founder · West Sahara Avenue · Since 2007

Dr. Peraza earned his medical degree at the University of Havana in 1991 and became a First-Degree Pediatric Specialist before earning his Nevada license in 2007. The same year, he opened Sahara Medical Institute around one idea: every conversation about a fever, a feeding question, a vaccine schedule, or a behavioral concern should happen in the language that family thinks in.

Nearly two decades on, the practice is three providers strong, accepting newborns and new patients, and still on West Sahara Avenue. Walk-ins for sick visits, telemedicine when you can't leave the house, and a team that practices the way he taught them: unhurried, thorough, and easy to understand.

What your first visit looks like

Before you come in

Bring three things.

Your insurance card, a photo ID, and your child's immunization record if you have one. For a transfer from another pediatrician, ask them to fax records to (702) 818-5013 ahead of time. Masks are required to enter the facility.

When you arrive

Front desk speaks Spanish.

Tell them whether you're here for a scheduled visit or a walk-in. For sick walk-ins, you'll see the first available provider on a first-come, first-served basis. You're welcome to request a specific provider if you'd rather schedule.

On your way out

You leave with a plan.

Whatever the visit is, you leave with the next step written down. Refill instructions in plain language, vaccine schedule on paper, a follow-up date if needed. We answer phone calls from established families for clarification afterward.
