A family practice, built for Vegas families.

Founded by Dr. Francisco Peraza so Spanish-speaking parents wouldn't have to choose between a doctor they trust and a doctor they can talk to.

Dr. Peraza opened Sahara Medical Institute in 2007 after years as a First-Degree Pediatric Specialist in Havana and a Nevada license earned the same year. He built the practice around one idea: the parent in the room should never feel translated. Every conversation about a fever, a feeding question, a vaccine schedule, or a behavioral concern happens in the language that family thinks in.

Nineteen years later, the practice is four providers strong, accepting newborns and new patients, and still on West Sahara Avenue. Walk-ins for sick visits, telemedicine when you can't leave the house, and a team that learned this from Dr. Peraza first: take the extra ten minutes, explain it again, write it down if it helps.
